Meowdoku
========

The first time
--------------
1. Double-click Meowdoku. Your Mac will say it can't check the app for
   malicious software, because the app isn't from the App Store.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Meowdoku. Enter your password
   if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   (On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click Meowdoku > Open > Open.)
3. If your Mac has an Apple chip (M1, M2, ...), it may ask to install
   Rosetta. Click Install.

After that, Meowdoku opens with a normal double-click. You can move it into
your Applications folder.

Using it
--------
1. Take a screenshot of the Meowdoku game on your phone and AirDrop it to
   your Mac. A game in progress is fine: your cats and X marks come along.
2. In Meowdoku, click "Open..." and choose the screenshot.
3. Click a square to cycle it: empty > X > cat.
   "Hint" explains the next logical step without giving away the answer.
   "Check" shows broken rules. "Solve" shows the answer.
